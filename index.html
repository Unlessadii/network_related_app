The Rural Connectivity Challenge — Solved

Over 60% of rural India experiences network dead zones, erratic 2G/3G throttles, and power cuts. Net Bypass transforms how rural citizens interact with digital services.

Offline-First Architecture

Every scheme, health facility, emergency number, and agricultural advisory is pre-cached on device SQLite storage. The app boots instantly in zero-network villages.

  • Instant startup (<1.4s) on sub-₹8,000 phones
  • Durable SQLite transactions
  • Zero blank screens or endless loading spinners

Idempotent Sync Queue

Post jobs, submit marketplace listings, and report fraud while completely offline. Mutations are safely enqueued in FIFO order and batch-synced once connection is detected.

  • Deterministic UUID transaction tracking
  • Zero duplicate listings during connection drops
  • Exponential retry backoff saves battery

Direct Cellular SOS

Life-safety emergency dialing (112, 108, 100, 181, 1098, 14567) connects directly through cellular telephony intents without requiring packet data or fake network promises.

  • Instant 1-tap dialing
  • Verified national emergency helplines
  • Transparent offline operation

Voice & Audio Accessibility

Designed for first-time smartphone users and semi-literate citizens. Spoken Text-To-Speech (TTS) reads out complex scheme benefits and farming advisories in natural cadence.

  • Multilingual intent parsing (Hindi & English)
  • One-tap speech synthesis readout
  • Large 48px+ high-contrast touch targets

100% Verified Schemes

Zero fake or hallucinated government welfare information. Strictly curated from official ministries: PM-Kisan, PMAY-G, Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY, PMFBY, and MGNREGA.

  • Direct official portal links (.gov.in / .nic.in)
  • Plain-language eligibility checklists
  • Required document guidance

Anti-Fraud Moderation

Automated screening blocks upfront fee scams, fake recruitment drives, illegal contraband, and counterfeit listings to protect vulnerable rural communities.

  • Auto-flagging on 3+ community reports
  • Admin review & sanction portal
  • Privacy-by-design data deletion
